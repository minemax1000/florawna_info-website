A complete guide to florawna

Continents

  • Cryosta

    • Cryosta lies at one of Florawna’s poles, locked in permanent frost and violent polar winds. From above, it looks like a dead, frozen wasteland — endless ice sheets, buried mountains, and no visible cities.

    But the surface is a lie.

    The World Below the Ice

    The people of Cryosta don’t live on the surface at all. They built their civilization underground, in vast cavern networks warmed by deep geothermal heat and ancient Aether currents flowing through the planet.

    These underground regions contain:

    • Crystal-lit cities carved into stone
    • Subterranean forests of pale, bioluminescent plants
    • Warm underground lakes and rivers

    The Fountain of Life

    At the deepest point beneath Cryosta, hidden below all known tunnels, lies a sealed sacred chamber.

    Here is the Fountain of Life

    A primordial spring of glowing, Aether-infused water.

    But here’s the impossible part:

    The Fountain pushes water up through natural channels in the planet’s crust. That water eventually:

    • Reaches the surface as Cryosta’s rare meltwater springs
    • Feeds underground rivers
    • Travels across Florawna
    • Becomes part of the global oceans

  • Nyxandra

    • Nyxandra is a land where life grows wild, deep, and ancient beyond measure. Dense forests stretch from coast to coast, layered in thick canopies that turn sunlight into a soft green glow. The air is warm, heavy with moisture, pollen, and Aether-rich vitality.

    The land is never still.

    Vines creep across ruins in days. Flowers bloom overnight. Roots slowly reshape the terrain. Insects shimmer like drifting embers, and distant calls of unseen creatures echo through the leaves. It feels less like walking on land and more like moving through the body of a living world.

    The Tree of Time

    At the heart of Nyxandra rises a colossal tree older than recorded history.

    Its trunk is wider than cities.

    Its crown disappears into the clouds.

    Its roots run so deep and far that entire ecosystems grow around them like veins around a heart.

    This is the Tree of Time

    It is not just a plant — it is a temporal anchor woven into the fabric of Florawna.

    Legends say:

    • The rings within its trunk mark not years, but ages
    • Its leaves fall when important events conclude
    • New branches form when new eras begin
    • Time flows steadily because the Tree stands undisturbed

    Creatures that live near it often grow unusually old. Forests around it feel timeless, as if the past lingers gently in the air.

    The Forests of Nyxandra

    The entire continent thrives under the Tree’s influence:

    • Towering megaflora with trunks like cliffs
    • Bioluminescent moss lighting the forest floor at night
    • Rivers tangled with root-bridges and hanging gardens
    • Animals with slow life cycles and deep instincts tied to seasonal rhythms

    Some groves are said to “remember” — reacting to footsteps, voices, or magic used long ago in the same place.

  • Lavinia

    • Cutting through the continent along its natural shape is a massive open divide known as the Sky Chasm.

    From the outer sea to the start of this divide, there are about 10 km of normal land. Then the terrain rises sharply into a powerful natural barrier.

    Between the stable land and the edge of the Sky Chasm stands a massive mountain range, formed long ago to seal and protect what lies beyond.

    These mountains are:

    • Steep and difficult to cross
    • Constantly battered by violent winds
    • Filled with narrow passes and ancient watchpoints

    But not everyone is meant to reach the Chasm.

    Only a few controlled paths lead through the mountains to the edge overlooking the floating interior.

    Inside the Chasm

    Beyond the mountains, the land suddenly ends, opening into a vast sky-filled abyss that follows the continent’s curve.

    Within it float:

    • Massive islands of rock and forest
    • Ruins of old structures
    • Platforms shaped for landing and travel

    Wind currents and spatial forces keep these islands suspended, creating a three-dimensional world of air, depth, and motion.

    The Trials of Entry

    Exam Part Type What It Tests
    Part I Written Exam Magical theory, spatial awareness, logic, problem-solving, and understanding how magic interacts with space.
    Part II Practical Trial Control of the candidate’s own magic, balance in strong winds, adaptability, and calmness in unstable environments near the Sky Chasm.

    It is also the resting place of a legendary artifact.

    The Book of Space

    It contains knowledge of:

    • How distance truly works
    • How space bends and connects
    • The hidden structure that allows portals and spatial magic to exist

  • Typhcrena

    • Typhcrena is a land of heat, ash, and living flame. Volcanoes rise like jagged towers across the continent, and rivers of lava carve glowing paths through blackened stone valleys.

    The sky often glows red or orange from distant eruptions, and the ground is warm even far from active volcanoes. Fire here is not just destruction — it is transformation.

    Plants grow in ash-rich soil. Creatures adapt to heat and smoke. Life exists here in fierce, resilient forms.

    The Flame of Death

    Deep within Typhcrena’s most ancient volcanic region there is a temple:

    Here lies The Flame of Death

    Despite its name, it is not a force of evil.

    This flame represents the moment of passing — the transition from physical life to the spirit world. It is the fire that releases, not the fire that destroys.

    What it does:

    • When a being dies naturally, their spiritual essence is said to pass through this flame
    • The Flame burns away the ties to the physical world
    • It guides souls safely onward instead of letting them become lost or trapped

    The Sacred Region

    The Flame of Death is located in a heavily protected volcanic caldera, surrounded by:

    • Fields of black glass-like obsidian
    • Geysers of steam and bursts of harmless spirit-flame
    • Stone temples built by those who revere the balance of life and death

    Only certain guardians, priests, or spirit-guides are allowed near the inner sanctum.

    Symbolism of Typhcrena

    Fire here means:

    • Endings that make way for new beginnings
    • Release from suffering
    • Transformation of matter into spirit

    In Typhcrena, death is not feared the same way. It is seen as a crossing, and the Flame ensures the path is clear.

  • Erilia

    • Erilia is the geographic heart of Florawna — vast, ancient, and impossibly diverse. Some scholars say that if someone crossed all of Erilia, they would experience a piece of every other continent within it.

    Across its enormous lands lie:

    • endless golden plains
    • towering mountain chains
    • deep primeval forests
    • wind-carved stone badlands
    • inland seas and vast river sistems

    But Erilia's true importance is not its size

    It is the only continent that holds both ends of existence. At opposite corners of it's land stand two cosmic gateways - one to the Void, and one to the Aether.

    Northeast - The Shadow Lagoon

    The Void Portal

    In Erilia’s far northeast lies a drowned region of black waters, twisted trees, and perpetual dim light. The air is heavy, still, and strangely muffled, as if sound itself fears to travel there.

    At the center of this region is the Shadow Lagoon — a perfectly circular body of dark, glasslike water.

    Nothing reflects on its surface.

    Not light.

    Not the sky.

    Not even magic.

    At its deepest point rests the Void Portal — not built, not summoned, but naturally formed where the barrier between worlds thinned long ago.

    From time to time:

    • ripples move across the water with no wind
    • faint violet fractures of light appear beneath the surface
    • strange entities are said to rise… or fall through

    The Lagoon is not ruled, not settled, and not studied for long. Those who linger too near often report:

    • feeling watched from below
    • hearing distant whispers with no source
    • sensing gravity pulling in the wrong direction

    This is not an invasion point. It is a pressure point — where the Void presses closest against reality.

    Southeast — Reflection Mountain

    The Aether Portal

    At the opposite edge of Erilia rises a single, breathtaking peak known as Reflection Mountain.

    Unlike normal stone, the mountain’s upper slopes shimmer faintly, as though made from crystal-veined rock that catches and bends light into soft halos. Snow never fully settles there — it melts into radiant mist before touching the ground.

    Near the summit stands a natural arch of luminous stone. Within it, space itself seems brighter, thinner, lighter.

    This is the Aether Portal.

    Where the Shadow Lagoon absorbs light, Reflection Mountain radiates it.

    Travelers near the peak often experience:

    • a deep sense of calm
    • memories of hope long forgotten
    • the feeling of being gently observed, not judged but understood

    Occasionally, beams of pale energy rise from the arch into the sky like silent auroras. Some believe these are:

    • souls returning to the Aether
    • blessings flowing into the world
    • or messages sent by the Guardians beyond

    Temples and sanctuaries have been built lower on the mountain, but none dare construct too close to the portal itself. The energy there is too pure, too overwhelming for mortal bodies to endure for long.

Islands

  • Eclipse Island

    • Located east of the Nyxandra coast, Eclipse Island is one of the oldest places in all of Florawna. Long before the current ages were counted, the island already existed as a place of power.

    Ancient temples lie scattered across its surface — broken, weathered, and partially reclaimed by nature. Their builders are unknown, and their purpose has been lost to time. Some are carved from pale stone that reflects light unnaturally, while others are formed from dark rock that seems to absorb it.

    Despite their age, the ruins remain strangely intact.

    The Triple Eclipse

    Eclipse Island is the only location in Florawna where all three moons can align perfectly with the sun.

    This phenomenon is known as The Eclipse Day:

    • It occurs once per season (every three months)
    • During this time, the sun and all three moons align
    • Light and shadow reach complete balance

    Nowhere else in the world does this alignment fully occur.

    The Beast of Eclipse Island

    No human or human-like being is able to set foot on Eclipse Island and for a good reason.

    The island is inhabited by an ancient beast. Its form has never been clearly recorded, as no human who has encountered it has survived. The beast does not attack animals — wildlife lives freely across the island, nesting among the ruins and roaming the land undisturbed.
